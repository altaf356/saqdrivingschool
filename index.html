FAQ

Questions Before You Start?

How many lessons do I need?

It depends on your current experience, confidence, and road test timeline. Beginners often benefit from a personalized plan after the first lesson.

Do you provide vehicles for road tests?

Contact SAQ Driving School to confirm vehicle availability and road test package options.

Do you teach nervous drivers?

Yes. Our nervous driver program is designed for calm, patient, step-by-step confidence building.

Can adults learn to drive?

Absolutely. Adult learners are welcome, including beginners, returning drivers, and international drivers.

Do you offer highway lessons?

Yes. Highway lessons help students practice merging, lane changes, speed control, and safe following distance.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Jamaica, Queens, and the greater NYC area, including Brooklyn, Bronx, Manhattan, Long Island, Nassau County, and Suffolk County.

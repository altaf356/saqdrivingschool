The route

One path, four stops, one license

Our core lessons follow a real progression — each stop builds on the last, so you arrive at your road test ready instead of rushed.

MM 01

Beginner Driving Lessons

For students with little or no experience behind the wheel.

  • Vehicle controls, steering, acceleration & braking
  • Lane positioning, turning, and parking basics
  • Traffic awareness and road safety fundamentals
MM 02

Intermediate Driving Lessons

For drivers ready to handle real traffic conditions.

  • Complex intersections and lane changes
  • Merging and city driving
  • Parking refinement
MM 03

Advanced Driving Lessons

For students preparing for higher-stakes driving situations.

  • Highway driving and heavy traffic management
  • Defensive driving and emergency response awareness
  • Long-distance driving preparation
MM 04

DMV Road Test Preparation

Our most-booked service — built to remove test-day surprises.

  • Mock road tests scored against real DMV criteria
  • Test route familiarity and common-mistake coaching
  • Final readiness assessment, plus a rental car for test day

Before you book

Common questions

How much do lessons cost?+
Pricing depends on the package and number of lessons you need. Call or message us and we'll recommend a plan based on your experience level and goals — no pressure, no hidden fees.
Do you offer lesson packages?+
Yes. Most students book a package that moves through beginner, intermediate, and advanced lessons before road test prep, though single lessons are available too.
Can I use your car for my road test?+
Yes — a properly insured and maintained vehicle is available for your DMV exam, so you test in a car you've already trained in.
How flexible is scheduling?+
Lessons are scheduled around work, school, and family commitments, including evenings and weekends where available.
What do I need to bring for the DMV road test?+
We'll walk you through current DMV documentation requirements before your scheduled test date so there are no surprises.
